How to update your site

Everything on the site comes from one editor. Change a photo or a sentence there, press Publish, and the site updates itself about a minute later. No code, no files, nothing to install.

Where things are

Your site

galleriaguzman.studio

The editor

galleriaguzman.studio/studio

Bookmark the editor link. Sign in with the same account you accepted the invite with. On the left you'll see three things: Site settings, Price list, and Galleries. That's the whole site.

Site settings

One page for the things that appear everywhere.

Name
Top-left of every page and the big title on the home page.
Tagline
One short line under your name on the home page.
Home page photos
The full-screen photos at the top of the home page. Add more than one and they become a slideshow. Drag to reorder.
About page photo, About text
The About page. Write it however you'd talk.
Email, Phone, Instagram
Contact page and the footer. Instagram is just the handle, no @.
Based in
Shows small under your name, e.g. Portland, OR.

Galleries

Each gallery is a tile on the home page and its own page. Weddings, Couples, Family, and so on. Add as many as you like, delete any you don't want.

To add a gallery

  1. Click Galleries, then the + button.
  2. Type a Title.
  3. Next to Web address, click Generate. That makes the page URL from the title.
  4. Add a Cover photo. This is the tile on the home page.
  5. Add Photos. Drag them into the order you want.
  6. Press Publish at the bottom right.

The other gallery fields

Top of page photos
Full-screen slideshow at the top of that gallery's page. Leave it empty and the cover photo is used.
Sections
Optional. Split a gallery into titled parts, like Arrival, Ceremony, Dinner, each with its own photos. Leave empty for one continuous run.
Details tab
Place, Year, and Film or camera show in small type under the gallery title. Order on home page decides tile order; lower numbers first.

Photos

  • Upload the full-size file. The site makes its own smaller copies for phones and laptops. You don't need to resize anything.
  • Set the focal point. Hover a photo and click the crop icon. Drag the circle to the part of the photo that matters most. Full-screen photos and home tiles get cropped to fit different screens, and this tells them what to keep.
  • Order is what you see. Drag photos up or down in the list to change their order on the page.
  • Portrait and landscape both work. Tall photos are shown a bit smaller so they don't take over the screen.

Price list

One page: an intro, your packages, and fine print. Each package has a name, a price written however you like ($1,800 or From $450), a one-line summary, and a list of what's included. The Pricing link in the menu only appears once at least one package is published, so you can take your time.

Publishing

Nothing goes live until you press Publish. Edits save on their own as drafts, so you can leave and come back. When it looks right, press Publish. The site rebuilds itself and the change is live in about a minute. If you don't see it, refresh the page once.

To undo something, open the document, click the three dots at the top right, and use the history there. Every version is kept.

Things that trip people up

  • Forgot to click Generate next to Web address. A gallery won't publish without one.
  • Deleting a photo from a gallery only removes it from that gallery. It's still in the media library if you want it elsewhere.
  • Site settings and Price list can't be deleted, only edited. That's on purpose.
  • Two addresses. galleriabriseno.studio also works and just sends people to galleriaguzman.studio.
